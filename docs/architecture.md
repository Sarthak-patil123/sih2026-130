# MAITRI 2.0 System Architecture

## Problem Statement: PS 26130 (Smart India Hackathon 2026)
**AI-Powered Integrated Business Approval & Compliance Operating System**
*Directorate of Industries, Government of Maharashtra*

---

## Core Pillars & Modules

### 1. Unified Entrepreneur Journey & Project Passport
- **Dynamic Project Passport**: Tracks corporate metadata, investment scale, MIDC zoning, and aggregate readiness score.
- **Visual Business Journey**: 7-stage linear & parallel pipeline from conceptualization to commercial operations.
- **Next Best Action Engine**: AI-assisted priority resolution to unblock statutory roadblocks.
- **Blocker Resolution Matrix**: Real-time dependency tracking across MIDC, MPCB, Fire, DISH, and SEIAA clearances.

### 2. Document Locker & Auto-Reuse Vault
- **One-Time Upload**: Cryptographically stored company documents (PAN, GST, CAD Site Blueprints, Title Deeds).
- **Auto-Demarcation**: Eliminates redundant document requests across disparate state nodal departments.

### 3. Government Officer Scrutiny Workdesk
- **Queue & SLA Countdown**: Live statutory tracking under the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act.
- **Objection & Query Resolution**: Direct technical query communication with applicants with upload request callbacks.
- **Joint Inspection Scheduling**: Coordinated multi-departmental field audits to prevent duplicate site inspections.

### 4. State Policy Lab & Executive Decision Support
- **Interactive 36-District Maharashtra Map**: Regional clearance velocity, throughput, and bottleneck radar.
- **What-If Policy Simulator**: Dynamic modeling of regulatory levers (inspection turnaround, auto-validation, parallel ratios).
- **SHA-256 Cryptographic Audit Trail**: Tamper-evident ledger architecture with Hyperledger integration blueprint.
